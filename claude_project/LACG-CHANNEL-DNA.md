## 0. CHANNEL META

- CHANNEL_NAME: Like a Chess Game
- NICHE: famous mind-game scenes from TV series and films, rated move by move like a chess game
- LANGUAGE: English (US) — cards, titles, descriptions, comments, subtitles
- NARRATION: none. No voiceover. Every word of commentary is an on-screen move card that types out over a frozen frame.
- VIDEO LENGTH: the clip (2-10 min) plus the freezes, usually 4-12 min
- LOOK: light theme — white side panel and white move cards with black text (the reference channel is dark-on-black). Move badges and class colours follow the familiar chess-app convention so viewers read them instantly.

## 1. TITLE PATTERNS

Every title ends with " | <Show or Film>" — the show name is the search anchor. Main character names come early. Never use "Analysed Like Chess" / "Analyzed Like Chess" (the reference channel's signature).

### GROUP A — VERSUS
"<A> vs <B>, Every Move Rated | <Show>"
"<A> vs <B> Is a Perfect Chess Game | <Show>"
"<A> vs <B>: Who Really Won? | <Show>"
The pure face-off. Best when both characters are famous.

### GROUP B — THE PLAY
"How <A> Outplays <B>, Move by Move | <Show>"
"<A> Sets the Perfect Trap | <Show>"
"<A> Lies to <B> and Wins | <Show>"
The key move or plan of one character. Best when one side clearly wins with a clever idea.

### GROUP C — THE VERDICT
"<A>'s Biggest Blunder | <Show>"
"The Most Brilliant Move in <Show>"
"<A> Never Saw This Checkmate Coming | <Show>"
A judgement on one move or one player. Best when there is a famous mistake or a famous brilliant moment.

Rules: max 70 characters, Title Case, no claim the video does not pay off (a "Brilliant" title needs a brilliant in the moves).

---

## 2. VIDEO STRUCTURE

1. Cold open: the clip simply starts — no intro card, no logo sting. The two player boxes and the eval bar are on screen from frame 1.
2. Moves: the clip plays; at each move the frame freezes, the badge pops (with its class sound), the eval bar slides, the card types out (typing sound), BETTER and TAG appear, a 2-3 s reading pause, then the clip continues from the same frame.
3. Subtitles of the dialogue run at the bottom the whole time (one speaker per subtitle line).
4. End: the Game Review table (accuracy, move counts per class for both sides, Opening name, Result) with the checkmate sound — held ~15 s so YouTube end-screen elements fit beside it.

---

## 3. SCENE SELECTION — CHESS-FIT TEST

A scene qualifies only if ALL are true:
1. Two clear sides (a side may be 2-3 characters acting together).
2. A duel of wits, not of fists: interrogation, negotiation, bluff, blackmail, trap, standoff, trial, card game, confession, a deal.
3. At least ~8 distinct turns (decisions) — enough for 8+ moves.
4. A turning point: one moment where the balance flips or is sealed.
5. 2-10 minutes long and mostly dialogue-driven (actions are fine as moves, but the game must be readable).
6. The show/film is widely watched and still searched (see CONTENT-BANK §4).

Bonus: a famous line the audience already quotes; a character known as a "chess player" (Gus, Lecter, Tywin, Logan Roy, Light Yagami, Columbo).

## 4. THUMBNAIL STYLE

- Built from the video's own frozen frames + move badges. No AI-generated faces, no stock images.
- Faces large and readable at phone size; one strong emotion per face.
- Layout split (two opponents, divider between) or single (one face).
- Badges are big and few: 1-2 per thumbnail.
- No text by default. Only a GROUP C thumbnail may carry 1-3 words, and only one of the three.

## 6. DIFFERENTIATION vs REFERENCE

| Axis | Reference (Analysed Like Chess) | Like a Chess Game |
|---|---|---|
| Look | Dark panel, white text on dark cards | Light theme: white panel, white cards, black text |
| Titles | "<X> Analysed like Chess \| <Show>" | Three title groups (Versus / The Play / The Verdict), never "Analysed like Chess" |
| Opening | Book moves only | Every game gets an invented OPENING name ("The Miranda Gambit"), shown on the first book move and in the Game Review |
| Turning point | Not marked | The move with the biggest eval swing is marked as the Turning Point in the Game Review |
| Scene pool | Breaking Bad / BCS / Dexter / The Wire heavy | Wider pool (CONTENT-BANK §4), fresh scenes first |

---

## 7. RIGHTS & REUSED-CONTENT SAFETY

Clip-based channels live with Content ID claims; the goal is to stay clearly transformative:
- The commentary must carry the video: freezes with cards spread evenly through the clip (STYLE-GUIDE §2 PACING — no run of more than ~35 s of untouched footage).
- Use only the scene, not the whole episode; 10 minutes of footage at most.
- Every video adds analysis that the footage alone does not contain: moves, classes, eval, review.
- No re-uploads of trailers, music videos, or scenes without analysis.
